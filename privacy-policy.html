Privacy Policy

Guitar Oracle is a free guitar practice and learning tool. We respect your privacy.

Guitar Oracle does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. We do not require user accounts, names, email addresses, passwords, payment details, contact information, or other personal data to use the site.

We do not use forms, advertising trackers, analytics services, cookies, or similar technologies to build user profiles. The tuner audio and metronome features run in your browser and are not used to identify or track you.

We do not knowingly collect information from children or any other users. We do not sell or disclose user information to third parties because we do not collect user information through Guitar Oracle.

Your browser or internet service provider may create standard technical records when accessing any website, and external services used to load site resources may have their own privacy practices. Those records are outside Guitar Oracle's control.

We may update this policy if the site's features change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page.
